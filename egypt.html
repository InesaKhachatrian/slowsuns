The route

Day by day

Eight days from Cairo up the Nile to Abu Simbel, by train, boat and car.

  1. 1
    Days 1–2

    Cairo and Giza

    See the pyramids at opening time, then the Grand Egyptian Museum nearby. Spend the evening in Khan el-Khalili.

    • Pyramids of Giza
    • Grand Egyptian Museum
    • Khan el-Khalili
  2. 3
    Days 3–4

    Luxor

    Overnight sleeper train or a 1-hour flight. Valley of the Kings at 6 am, Karnak in the afternoon, Luxor Temple lit up after dark.

    • Valley of the Kings
    • Karnak Temple
    • Luxor Temple
  3. 5
    Days 5–6

    Up the Nile to Aswan

    A short cruise, or a car with stops at Edfu and Kom Ombo. In Aswan, take the boat to Philae Temple.

    • Edfu Temple
    • Kom Ombo Temple
    • Philae Temple
  4. 7
    Day 7

    Abu Simbel

    About 3.5 hours each way by road from Aswan, leaving before dawn; a short flight is the easy option.

    • Abu Simbel temples
  5. 8
    Day 8

    Back to Cairo

    Fly from Aswan to Cairo and leave a buffer before your flight home.

    • Nubian village, Aswan (morning)

Tips

Good to know

Tips

  • Start the Valley of the Kings at opening time, before the heat and the tour buses.
  • Carry small notes for tips; they are expected almost everywhere.
  • Cover shoulders and knees, and bring a scarf for mosques.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Following someone who says a sight is closed and offers to show you another one.
  • Drinking tap water: buy bottled.
  • Booking a tight connection on the day you visit Abu Simbel.
