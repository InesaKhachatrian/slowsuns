The route

Day by day

Four days on foot from one hotel, with a day trip by train.

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Old Town and the Jewish Quarter

    Watch the Astronomical Clock strike the hour once, then walk the Jewish Quarter: one ticket covers its synagogues and the Old Jewish Cemetery.

    • Old Town Square
    • Astronomical Clock
    • Jewish Quarter
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Charles Bridge and Prague Castle

    Cross Charles Bridge before 8 am, then walk up to the castle. The circuit ticket covers St Vitus Cathedral and Golden Lane.

    • Charles Bridge
    • Prague Castle
    • St Vitus Cathedral
    • Golden Lane
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Malá Strana, Petřín and Vyšehrad

    Wander Malá Strana and the Lennon Wall, walk up Petřín Hill to the tower, and finish at Vyšehrad for the river view at sunset.

    • Lennon Wall
    • Petřín Tower
    • Vyšehrad
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Day trip to Kutná Hora

    About an hour by train (off the map). See the bone chapel at Sedlec Ossuary and St Barbara's Church, then head back for a last dinner in Prague.

    • Sedlec Ossuary
    • St Barbara's Church

Tips

Good to know

Tips

  • When a card machine offers euros, choose to pay in koruna: the rate is better.
  • Buy a 24- or 72-hour transport pass and validate it once.
  • Be on Charles Bridge before 8 am for photos without the crowds.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Changing money at "0 % commission" booths: their rates are poor.
  • Eating on Old Town Square, where prices are highest.
  • Leaving the castle for the afternoon, when the queues are longest.
