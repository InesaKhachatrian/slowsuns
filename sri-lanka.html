The route

Day by day

Ten days in a loop: old kingdoms in the north, tea hills in the middle, beaches in the south.

  1. 1
    Days 1–2

    Sigiriya and Dambulla

    About 4 hours by car from the airport. Climb Sigiriya when it opens at 7 am, before the heat; Pidurangala Rock next door has the best view of it at sunset.

    • Sigiriya Rock
    • Pidurangala Rock
    • Dambulla Cave Temple
  2. 3
    Days 3–4

    Kandy

    Visit the Temple of the Tooth for the evening ceremony, and spend a slow morning in the Peradeniya botanical gardens.

    • Temple of the Tooth
    • Peradeniya Botanical Gardens
    • Kandy Lake
  3. 5
    Days 5–6

    Train to Ella

    The hill-country train takes about 7 hours through the tea estates. In Ella, walk to the Nine Arch Bridge and up Little Adam's Peak.

    • Kandy–Ella train
    • Nine Arch Bridge
    • Little Adam's Peak
  4. 7
    Days 7–8

    Mirissa

    About 3 hours by car down to the coast. Whale-watching boats run from about November to April; keep an afternoon for Coconut Tree Hill.

    • Mirissa Beach
    • Coconut Tree Hill
    • Whale watching
  5. 9
    Days 9–10

    Galle Fort, then home

    Walk the fort ramparts at sunset, then take the expressway to the airport, about 2 to 2.5 hours.

    • Galle Fort ramparts
    • Galle Lighthouse

Tips

Good to know

Tips

  • Book reserved seats on the Kandy–Ella train as early as you can; they sell out.
  • Cover shoulders and knees at temples, and take off shoes and hats.
  • Plan short driving days: 100 km can take 3 hours or more.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Planning the south coast for May to September, when the monsoon hits it.
  • Posing for photos with your back to a Buddha statue: it is seen as disrespectful.
  • Packing every day full; the heat makes afternoons slow.
