The route

Day by day

Four days from one base in Split, with day trips by bus and boat.

  1. 1
    Day 1

    Diocletian's Palace and Marjan

    Start at the Peristyle around 8 am, before the tour groups. Climb the cathedral bell tower, walk the Riva, and end the day on Marjan Hill for sunset.

    • Peristyle
    • Cathedral of St Domnius
    • Riva promenade
    • Marjan Hill
  2. 2
    Day 2

    Krka National Park

    About 1 h 15 min by car or bus. Walk the boardwalk loop around Skradinski buk; swimming at the falls is no longer allowed.

    • Skradinski buk
    • Skradin
  3. 3
    Day 3

    Hvar by catamaran

    About an hour each way. Climb to the Fortica for the view over the harbour, then take a taxi boat to the Pakleni Islands for a swim.

    • Hvar Town
    • Fortica fortress
    • Pakleni Islands
  4. 4
    Day 4

    Brač and Zlatni Rat

    Ferry to Supetar, then the bus across the island to Bol. Zlatni Rat is busiest at midday, so arrive early or stay for the evening.

    • Supetar
    • Bol
    • Zlatni Rat beach

Tips

Good to know

Tips

  • Buy Krka tickets online in summer; the busiest entry times sell out.
  • Book catamaran tickets to Hvar a day ahead in July and August.
  • Bring water shoes: most beaches here are pebbles.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Driving into the old town: it is pedestrian-only, so park outside.
  • Visiting the palace at midday in August, when it's hottest and busiest.
  • Taking the last catamaran back with no time to spare.
